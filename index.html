🤖 What Is Third-Party Risk Intel?
The 30-second pitch

Third-Party Risk Intel is an AI agent that investigates your third parties for you. You give it a company name — it resolves the entity, maps ownership structures, screens against sanctions, PEPs, watchlists, and adverse media, and delivers a citation-backed risk narrative in minutes, not hours.

What normally takes 2–4 hours takes TPRI 4 minutes. It surfaces an average of 3+ hidden ownership connections per third party, eliminates false positives, and produces a fully auditable report.

The 3-Tier Model
#ProductRole

TPRI qualifies risk earlier and sends better, citation-backed cases into DDS — so analyst capacity is reserved for what actually needs it.

💬 Objection Handling & FAQ
"We already have a screening provider (Refinitiv/D&B)"
They provide data; we provide answers. Those tools dump thousands of hits on you. TPRI automates the 2–4 hours of manual research your team does after getting those hits to determine what's real.
"How is this different from your existing AI features?"
Legacy AI summarized static screening results. Third-Party Risk Intel maps the corporate structure before screening, discovering risk hidden in ownership that standard tools miss entirely.
"Is this using my data to train a public model?"
Absolutely not. The system uses a RAG architecture. Your data is isolated in your tenant and is never used to train shared models. AWS Bedrock does not store or log prompts and completions for model training.
"What happens if the AI misses something?"
TPRI is a human-in-the-loop system. It provides citations for every finding. It's an efficiency tool to clear low/medium risks — not a replacement for your final judgment on high-risk entities.
"My 3PM client enabled AI Screening but users can't see it."
Out-of-box roles do NOT include AI Screening permissions. The admin must create a custom role with AI Screening access and assign it to the relevant users.
"My EU client says 'No searches remaining' even though they just enabled it."
Known display issue. Workaround: go to AI Services settings, change the default value from 100 to 101, and click Save. Cosmetic only — searches are available. Permanent fix coming in a future release.
"Can a client have TPRI on both D1P and 3PM?"
Yes — 100 (3PM) + 50 (D1P) = 150 total during trial. We allow this to maximize feedback, but guide customers toward D1P for the richer experience.
"Can a 3PM customer without D1P try full TPRI?"
Yes — any customer can get a new D1P org provisioned. $0 Trial Order Form, Entitlements sets up a fresh D1P tenant within 1–3 business days.
"Does TPRI access my existing Diligent system data?"
No. TPRI only processes the search subject entered by the user. It does not touch questionnaires, workflows, approvals, cases, or other internal records. Admin opt-in required before anything runs.
"The pricing seems high."
Consider the current process: analyst time at $75/hr × 2–4 hours × 500 entities = $75K–$150K annually. TPRI pays for itself on time savings alone, before factoring in risk reduction. Sanctions breaches carry average costs exceeding $14M per event.
